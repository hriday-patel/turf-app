Delete your FieldPass Business account

FieldPass Business · Account & data deletion request

You can permanently delete your FieldPass Business account and all your data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Heads up: account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. It removes your owner profile, all your turfs, all bookings made on your turfs, and your uploaded images.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (instant)

  1. Open the FieldPass Business app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the Account section at the bottom.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Type DELETE in the confirmation box and tap Delete forever.

Your account is removed immediately.

Option 2 — Email a deletion request (no app required)

If you can no longer access the app, send an email from the email address you registered with to:

avioragloballlp@gmail.com

Include the following in your email:

  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request
  • The phone number associated with your owner account (so we can verify it's you)
  • (Optional) the name of one of your turfs, to help us locate the account quickly

We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your owner profile (name, email, phone, profile photo)
  • All turfs you have listed (turf details, photos, slot configurations, holiday calendars)
  • All bookings made on your turfs (player names, phone numbers, slot times, payment status)
  • Your authentication record (password, Google OAuth link, login session)
  • All files you uploaded to FieldPass storage

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Backups are rotated and overwritten within 30 days.
