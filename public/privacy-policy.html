Privacy Policy

FieldPass Business · Last updated: 23 April 2026

FieldPass Business ("we", "our", "the app") is a turf-management application for sports-facility owners. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we store it, and how you can request its deletion.

1. Data we collect

  • Account info: name, email address, phone number, and (optionally) a profile photo. Collected when you sign up; required to create and authenticate your account.
  • Authentication data: password (stored hashed by Supabase Auth), Google OAuth tokens, one-time SMS codes. Used only to log you in.
  • Turf data: turf name, type, address, city, photos, opening hours, slot durations, pricing rules, and a list of public holidays. Collected when you list a turf.
  • Booking data: bookings created on your turfs, including player name, player phone, slot times, advance amount, payment status, and transaction IDs (where applicable).
  • App activity: actions you take in the app such as creating turfs, blocking slots, and updating bookings — stored so the app can show you your history.
  • Crash diagnostics: none. We do not currently ship Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry, or any third-party analytics SDK.
  • Advertising IDs: we do not collect or use the Android Advertising ID. We do not show ads.

2. Where your data is stored

All app data is stored in Supabase, hosted in Supabase's cloud infrastructure. Backend API calls are routed through Vercel serverless functions. WhatsApp message delivery uses the WhatsApp Cloud API operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.

All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS. On your device, your login session is stored in the operating system's hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).

3. How we use your data

  • To create, authenticate, and operate your owner account.
  • To display your turfs, slots, and bookings inside the app.
  • To send booking-confirmation messages to the players who book your turfs (via WhatsApp).
  • To diagnose user-reported errors when you share a screenshot or session info with our support.

4. Data we do NOT collect

  • Precise GPS location.
  • Contacts, calendar, microphone, or call logs.
  • Files outside of images you explicitly pick from the system gallery.
  • Health, fitness, financial-instrument (card / bank) data, web browsing history, or any sensitive demographic data.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your personal data with advertising networks, data brokers, or any third party for marketing purposes. The third-party processors below are used only to operate the service:

  • Supabase — database + authentication + file storage.
  • Vercel — serverless backend hosting.
  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API — to deliver booking-confirmation messages.
  • Google — only when you choose "Sign in with Google" (your Google account email is provided to us by Google).

6. Account deletion & data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data in two ways:

  1. From inside the app: Profile Settings → Account → Delete Account. This action is immediate and irreversible.
  2. Without the app: visit /delete-account and follow the instructions to email a deletion request from your registered email address.

When you delete your account, the following are removed within 30 days from all production systems and backups: your owner profile, your turfs, your slot configurations, all bookings on your turfs, your uploaded images, and your authentication record.

7. Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and notify users in-app on next launch.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data-access / data-deletion requests by email, contact support@fieldpass.app.
